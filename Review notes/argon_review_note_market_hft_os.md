## 3. Required Corrections and Verification Tasks (Pending Owner Authorization)

1. **Cryptographic & Promotion Gate Hardening (`ARGON-CRYPTO-001`, `ARGON-CRYPTO-002`, `ARGON-EVID-001`)**:
   - In `src/verification/gate.ts` and `src/persistence/atomicPromotion.ts`, require `rcpt.claim_signing_payload_sha256` to match the full `ClaimSigningPayload` SHA-256 (`sha256(canonicalizeJCS(validateAndBuildClaimSigningPayload(offer.offer_id, offer.product_id, claim)))`) and verify `rcpt.operator_id === claim.attestation.attester_id` and `rcpt.operator_role === claim.attestation.claimed_role`.
   - In `src/verification/claims.ts` (`validateAttesterAuthority`), move `if (principal.compromised)` outside `if (principal.revoked)` so compromised keys always fail closed (`E-ERR-019`), and require `principal.revoked_at_utc` to be a valid timestamp when `principal.revoked === true` (throwing `E-ERR-019` if missing or invalid).
   - Extend `ResolvedEvidenceEnvelope` with the 4D evidence attributes (`evidence_kind`, `verification_status`, `offer_status`, `document_type`) and invoke `validateEvidenceAllowlistEligibility` inside `evaluateOfferPromotionReadOnly` and `commitPromotionIfVersionAndConsumeNonces`.
2. **Currency, FX & Financial Engine Remediation (`ARGON-FIN-001`, `ARGON-FIN-002`)**:
   - In `src/canonical/currency.ts`, validate `source` via `createCanonicalMoney`, validate `rateDecimal` against a strict positive decimal regex with exact `rateScale` fractional digit scaling, and verify `receipt.oracle_public_key_hex` against an explicit trusted oracle authority registry (checking key validity window, revocation, and compromise).
   - Refactor `src/financial/cashFlowEngine.ts` to use `CanonicalMoney` / `bigint` subunits (or safe integers bound to an explicit `CurrencyCode`), validate all entry timestamps with `validateIso8601UtcTimestamp`, sort chronologically by `(Date.parse(entry_timestamp_utc), sequence_number)`, implement the Two-Tier Gross-Clearing model (`B_clearing` vs `B_bank`), validate `CommittedCapitalManager` inputs/states (including `amendPO` and rejecting operations on `FULFILLED` POs), and implement the specified XIRR / polynomial NPV solver with all 6 status codes.
3. **Persistence, WAL & Simulation Pipeline Remediation (`ARGON-PERSIST-001`, `ARGON-PERSIST-002`, `ARGON-SIM-001`)**:
   - In `src/persistence/durableRecoveryJournal.ts`, compute `validBytesLength` from exact byte offsets in `fileBuffer`, verify hash-chain integrity on valid prefix entries before truncating a torn tail, fail closed on constructor normalization errors (or check `journal.getIsPoisoned()` at the start of `CrashRecoveryEngine.recoverDurable` before mutating `portfolio`), and merge the Boundary 3 check into a single chronological pass over `recordedFills`.
   - In `src/simulation/simulationPipeline.ts` and `src/execution/engine.ts`, track and decrement remaining offer quantity across fills, include a monotonic sequence/event identifier in `intent_id` to prevent same-millisecond tick collisions, compute `supplierIntent.canonical_intent_sha256` via `sha256(canonicalizeJCS(supplierIntent.payload))`, verify `intent.canonical_intent_sha256` inside `SimulatedExecutionGateway.submitExecutionIntent`, check `offer.offer_status === 'VERIFIED_EXECUTABLE_OFFER'` in `registerVerifiedOffer`, use `divideHalfEven` for `weighted_cost_basis_subunits`, and include full canonical journals/events/orders in `computeStateDigest()`.
   - Add `"scripts/**/*"` to `tsconfig.json` and align `scripts/test_crash_worker.ts` with `SimulatedFillEvent` and `SimulatedOrderRecord`.
